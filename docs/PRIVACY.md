# Privacy policy — SA-Sur

SA-Sur is a Mac app released by **Hafod Mastering Ltd** and **Sudeep Audio.com LLP** together. This page describes what the app does with your data.

The short version: **nothing leaves your Mac.**

*Applies to SA-Sur 1.0 and later. Last updated: 6 October 2026.*

## What the app collects

Nothing. SA-Sur has no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no accounts and no third-party frameworks. The app makes no network connections of its own.

## What the app stores

One setting, on your Mac only: your appearance preference (System, Light or Dark). It lives in the app's own preferences and is never transmitted anywhere.

## The links in the window

The window shows the two studios' marks. Clicking one asks your web browser to open that studio's website. That is an ordinary visit to that website, under that website's own privacy terms — the app itself sends nothing.

## Changes

If this policy ever needs to change, the current version will always be at this address in the app's repository.
