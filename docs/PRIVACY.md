# Privacy policy - SA-Sur

SA-Sur is released by **Hafod Mastering Limited** and **Sudeep Audio.com LLP** together, for macOS and Windows. This page describes what the app does with your data.

The short version: **nothing leaves your machine.**

*Applies to SA-Sur 1.0 and later, on every channel: the GitHub download, the Mac App Store and the Microsoft Store. Last updated: 20 October 2026.*

## What the app collects

Nothing. SA-Sur has no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no accounts and no tracking SDKs. The macOS build uses only Apple frameworks. The Windows build uses open-source UI and audio libraries (Avalonia, NAudio and their dependencies), which make no network connections. The app makes no network connections of its own.

The Microsoft Store declares the `runFullTrust` capability because this is a desktop app that plays audio through the system output device; that declaration is not a data collection. The app still collects nothing.

## What the app stores

One setting, on your machine only: your appearance preference (System, Light or Dark). It lives in the app's own preferences and is never transmitted anywhere.

## The links in the window

The window shows the two companies' marks. Clicking one asks your web browser to open that company's website. That is an ordinary visit to that website, under that website's own privacy terms - the app itself sends nothing.

## Changes

If this policy ever needs to change, the current version will always be at this address in the app's repository.
